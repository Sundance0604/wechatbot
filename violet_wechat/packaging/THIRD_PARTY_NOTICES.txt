Violet 0.8.1 bundles Python 3.11 and third-party runtime components.

The executable contains, among other dependencies: FastAPI, Starlette, Uvicorn,
HTTPX, Pydantic, psutil, Frida, wechatauto-replica 1.2.1 (database module only),
cryptography, zstandard, tzdata, certifi, h11, AnyIO, and their dependencies.
Their original license and notice files are included under licenses/ inside the
PyInstaller bundle. A dependency manifest accompanies the release archive.

PyInstaller's bootloader is distributed under its bootloader exception.
Python is distributed under the Python Software Foundation License.

WeChat, DeepSeek Harness, and Codex are not bundled. They remain separate
products installed and configured by the recipient. This is an unofficial
local tool and is not affiliated with Tencent, DeepSeek, or OpenAI.
